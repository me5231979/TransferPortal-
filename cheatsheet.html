The Transfer Portal, the quick reference

What it is

Vanderbilt's staff-facing front door for internal career movement, powered by Oracle. Inside: internal requisitions (five-business-day internal-first window), Careers of Interest, your skills profile, Grow plans, Gigs (not yet launched), and advisory support. Leaders see aggregated, privacy-preserving data through Workforce Intelligence.

The six entrances

  1. A · Explorer: curious, staying put. Careers of Interest + skills profile.
  2. B · New challenge: plateaued high performer. Gigs + Careers of Interest.
  3. C · Unused skills: grew past the role. Skills update + requisition search.
  4. D · Post-reorg: role feels misaligned. Advisory conversation + requisitions.
  5. E · At risk: restructure looming. Advisory + priority matching.
  6. F · Direct applicant: knows the job. Straight to the requisition.

What you control

  • Your profile and skill levels (tag them honestly).
  • Your Careers of Interest, held privately.
  • Your Gig and requisition applications.
  • Your disclosure timing: no announcement needed to explore; a professional conversation with your manager once you're a serious candidate.

HR guarantees

  • Privacy by default; interest data rolls up only in aggregate.
  • No auto-notification to your manager, ever.
  • Two-week internal-first window on eligible requisitions.
  • Advisory support on request, and only on request.

Managers commit to

  • No retaliation: blocking, rating threats, and sidelining are serious violations.
  • No surveillance: no workarounds to see who declared interest.
  • Coaching: hold the "what's next for you here" conversation early.
  • Respectful handoffs (typically 2 to 4 weeks) and backfill partnership.

The manager script, for the moment it matters

"Thank you for telling me. Tell me what drew you to it. What can I do to help you compete well, and whatever the outcome, what should we plan for next in your development here?"

The line the culture cannot survive: "After everything I've invested in you?"

The numbers, for the skeptics

  • Promoted within 3 years: 70% stay odds vs 45% for the unmoved (SHRM / LinkedIn).
  • High-mobility organizations: tenure 5.4 vs 2.9 years (Inop.ai).
  • Internal hires: 20 to 25% faster to full competency (LinkedIn).
  • External hiring: up to 3 to 5x the all-in cost (Bersin).
  • ~75% of managers self-report talent hoarding (Haegele, AER 2024).

Gigs, in one paragraph

A short-term stretch assignment in another unit: defined scope, defined duration, home role kept. Your manager approves it before it starts. It counts as development and never promises a permanent move; what it builds (skills, relationships, evidence) makes you a stronger candidate when a real requisition opens. One caveat: Gigs have not launched yet at Vanderbilt, so learn the concept now and watch for the announcement.

Do this today, five moves

  1. The growth conversation: with your manager about your growth; managers, with a team member.
  2. Transfer Portal: build your plan, or as a manager evaluate your team's skills.
  3. Career Preferences: update them in your Talent Profile in Oracle (next move, preferred function and family, aspirations).
  4. Skills: in the Skills Center, make sure Skills I have and Skills I'm developing are current and honestly leveled.
  5. Careers of Interest: flag them in the Grow area of Oracle, then browse the Opportunity Marketplace with Opportunity set to Career Roles.
