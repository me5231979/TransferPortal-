The job aid: your journey, mapped

Five small moves, in order. Nothing to submit; take them when you are ready. Career interest and skill declarations stay private to you.

1

The growth conversation

Talk with your manager about your growth. Managers: hold that conversation with a team member.

2

The Transfer Portal

Map your skills and build a growth plan. Managers: evaluate the skills across your team.

Open the Transfer Portal.

3

Career Preferences

In your Oracle Talent Profile: next career move, preferred function and family, aspirations in the comments.

Open your Talent Profile (Oracle lists it as Skills and Qualifications).

4

Skills

Both lists, Skills I have and Skills I'm developing, honestly leveled. The matching runs on this data.

Open the Skills Center.

5

Careers of Interest

In the Grow area, flag the roles that interest you, then browse the Opportunity Marketplace with Opportunity set to Career Roles: careers show with or without an open requisition.

Browse the Opportunity Marketplace.
