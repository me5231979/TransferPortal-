What it is

One private portal. Two paths.

The portal lives in Oracle and does two things.

1 · Career moves

New roles at Vanderbilt

Find open Vanderbilt jobs and compete for them. Staff see new postings first, for five business days. Flag the careers that interest you, and the portal matches roles to your skills. Private until you apply.

2 · Gigs

Short-term opportunities

Short projects in other units that build your skills. We are rolling Gigs out in Oracle to promote short-term opportunities. Your manager approves each Gig, you keep your home job, and a Gig never promises a transfer.

The promises

You control what you share. Showing interest alerts no one. Leaders see only group totals, never your activity. Managers may not punish or spy. Advisor help starts only when you ask.

Why it matters

People who can grow inside, stay.

That is good for careers and good for Vanderbilt. The research is clear.

Who it's for

Six common reasons people walk in.

Yours might be a mix. Every one starts best with a growth conversation with your manager, and exploring stays private until you say otherwise.

A · Curious

"I'm happy here, but what else is out there?" Browse privately; nobody is alerted.

B · Ready to stretch

"I need a new challenge." Take a Gig, keep your home job, build new skills.

C · Skills unused

"I can do more than my job asks." Tag your real skills and let matching find the fit.

D · After a reorg

"My job changed under me." Talk with a career advisor, privately, then apply where it fits.

E · Role at risk

"My position may go away." Get priority matching and faster review, on your request.

F · Ready to apply

"There's an open job I want." Apply during the staff-first window and win the interview.
