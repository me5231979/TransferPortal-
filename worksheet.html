My first move card

The paper version of the capstone: one direction, one move in the portal, one date. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · My direction

What are you curious about or ready for at Vanderbilt? A role family, a skill, a unit, or for managers, a team member's growth.

2 · My first move

Circle one: update my skills profile and declare a Career of Interest · browse and save a Gig · search internal requisitions · request an employee-consultant conversation · manager: hold the "what's next for you here" conversation. Why this move first?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: wait to be discovered · inflate my skill ratings · stall the career conversation · guilt or slow-walk a team member's move.

4 · My entrance

Which door fits today: A Explorer · B New challenge · C Unused skills · D Post-reorg · E At risk · F Direct applicant? What makes it yours?

5 · The date

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Put it on your calendar before you leave the room.

6 · The evidence line

Fill in after the move: what did you find in the portal, in one line?

7 · The disclosure plan

You owe nobody an announcement for exploring. If you become a serious candidate for something: when and how will you have the professional conversation with your manager?
